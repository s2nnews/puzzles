Where the money goes — net sales to contribution profit

Every dollar of the period's $6,182 net sales, drained by the real costs of doing business. What's left — contribution profit — is what pays overheads (rent, software, your time) and funds growth. Cost of goods is extrapolated from the 37% margin measured on the ~54% of sales with cost data loaded.
Bottom line: after making and shipping the puzzles, boxing them and buying the traffic, you kept about $1,223 this period — roughly $13 per order, a 19.8% contribution margin. That's the number to grow. The two biggest levers are cost of goods (load supplier costs on the other ~46% of SKUs so this stops being an estimate) and the shipping bleed (you eat ~$5.51 a parcel).

Make it live — from snapshot to always-on website

How this becomes a live dashboard you can bookmark

Right now this page is a fixed snapshot — the numbers are baked in at the moment it was generated. Three steps turn it into a self-updating website with a working date-range picker.
1 · The data store (already built). The Google Apps Script we set up writes one row per day — sales, units, funnel, subscribers, Meta spend, shipping cost/charged — into a Google Sheet, refreshed automatically every morning. That daily grain is the key: any date range is just a sum or filter over those rows, so no live API call is needed when you move the picker.
2 · Publish the data. The Sheet is published as a read-only feed (a single JSON/CSV endpoint). The dashboard fetches it on load instead of using baked-in numbers.
3 · Host the page. The HTML goes on a free static host (Cloudflare Pages / Netlify / GitHub Pages) at a URL you bookmark, or is served straight from Apps Script as a web app. The date-range picker and the previous-period comparison then compute live from the daily rows.
Net effect: you open one link, pick any range, and every tile, the funnel and the profit waterfall recompute from data that refreshed at breakfast — no exports, no rebuilds.
